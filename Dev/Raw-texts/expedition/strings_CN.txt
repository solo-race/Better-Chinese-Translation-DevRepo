In order to select a mission, you must first unlock all of its requirements. Some quests will require that a mission is completed to grant its rewards. You can also repeat a mission at anytime to strive for the fastest time!|若要选择一项使命，你必须先解锁它的所有要求。有些任务需要完成使命才能获得奖励。你也可以随时重复一项使命，争取以最快的时间完成它！
